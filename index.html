        How it works

        A book line is played until your opponent leaves the book with a different move, often an inaccuracy or a mistake. Find the best reply by moving a piece on the board. Stockfish judges your answer.

          How answers are rated

          Stockfish evaluates the position after your move and compares it with its own best move. Evaluations are in pawns, from your side: +1.0 means you are about a pawn ahead (or have an advantage worth that much), −1.0 means your opponent is. A knight or bishop is worth about 3 pawns, a rook 5, a queen 9. M3 means a forced mate in 3.
